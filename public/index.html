Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have got clear answers for you.

Who can participate?
Students studying in classes from standard 1st to 12th across any recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, State Board) are eligible to participate.
Is there negative marking?
No, there is zero negative marking in the AMIT Maths Olympiad examination, encouraging students to attempt all challenging questions with confidence.
Can I participate next year?
Yes, active students can participate every year according to their corresponding academic grade level.
How will results be announced?
Results are declared directly on our secure portal and official Telegram channel with downloadable merit rank cards.
