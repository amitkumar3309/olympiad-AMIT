### 1. `math-power-sum` — reviewed

If $x$ is a positive real number such that $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3$, what is the value of $\dfrac{x^5 + x^{-5}}{x^2 + x^{-2}}$?

- A. $\frac{115}{7}$
- B. $\frac{119}{7}$
- C. $\frac{123}{7}$ **← correct**
- D. $\frac{127}{7}$

**Why:** Squaring and cubing give $x^2 + x^{-2} = 7$ and $x^3 + x^{-3} = 18$, so $x^5 + x^{-5} = 7 \times 18 - 3 = 123$.

### 2. `math-odd-sum` — reviewed

What is the sum of the first 20 odd numbers, $1 + 3 + 5 + \dots$?

- A. 200
- B. 210
- C. 400 **← correct**
- D. 420

**Why:** The first $n$ odd numbers always add up to $n^2$, and $20^2 = 400$.

### 3. `math-divisors` — reviewed

How many positive whole numbers divide 360 exactly?

- A. 20
- B. 24 **← correct**
- C. 30
- D. 36

**Why:** $360 = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5$, so it has $(3+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 24$ divisors.

### 4. `math-area-change` — reviewed

**Why:** The new area is $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ times the old one — 4% smaller.

### 5. `math-last-digit` — reviewed

### 6. `math-average` — reviewed
