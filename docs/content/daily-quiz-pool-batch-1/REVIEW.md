- A. No profit, no loss ✅
- B. 45% profit

## Class 9 — `daily-quiz-pool-class-09.csv`

**1. If $x = 2 + \sqrt{3}$, what is $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$?**  
*Number Systems*

- B. 12

- D. 16

> $\frac{1}{x} = 2 - \sqrt{3}$, so $x + \frac{1}{x} = 4$. Then $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 4^2 - 2 = 14$.

**2. If $p(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$, what is $p(2) - p(1) + p\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$?**  
*Polynomials*

- A. $-\frac{1}{4}$
- B. $\frac{1}{4}$ ✅
- C. $\frac{5}{4}$
- D. $0$

> $p(2) = 4 - 8 + 3 = -1$, $p(1) = 1 - 4 + 3 = 0$ and $p\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4} - 2 + 3 = \frac{5}{4}$. So $-1 - 0 + \frac{5}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$.

**3. Written as $a + b\sqrt{15}$, what is $\frac{\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{3}}$?**  
*Number Systems*

- A. $4 + \sqrt{15}$ ✅
- B. $8 + 2\sqrt{15}$
- C. $4 - \sqrt{15}$
- D. $1 + \sqrt{15}$

> Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{3}$: $\frac{5 + 3 + 2\sqrt{15}}{5 - 3} = \frac{8 + 2\sqrt{15}}{2} = 4 + \sqrt{15}$.

**4. How many pairs of positive whole numbers $(x, y)$ satisfy $2x + 3y = 20$?**  
*Linear Equations in Two Variables*

- A. 3 ✅
- B. 2
- C. 4
- D. 6

> $3y$ must be even and less than 20, so $y = 2, 4, 6$, giving $x = 7, 4, 1$. Three pairs.

**5. A triangle has sides 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm. How long is the altitude drawn to the 14 cm side?**  
*Heron's Formula*

- A. 11 cm
- B. 12 cm ✅
- C. 13 cm
- D. 84 cm

> By Heron's formula, $s = 21$ and the area is $\sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = 84$. Altitude $= \frac{2 \times 84}{14} = 12$ cm.

**6. A chord subtends an angle of 100° at the centre of a circle. What angle does it subtend at a point on the major arc?**  
*Circles*

- A. 50° ✅
- B. 80°
- C. 130°

> The angle at the centre is twice the angle at any point on the remaining (major) arc: $100° \div 2 = 50°$.

*Statistics*

**3. How many terms of the AP 9, 17, 25, ... must be added to get a sum of 636?**

- B. 10
- C. 12 ✅
- D. 11

> $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[18 + 8(n - 1)] = n(4n + 5) = 636$. So $4n^2 + 5n - 636 = 0$, and $n = 12$.

**4. For what value of $k$ do $2x + 3y = 7$ and $4x + ky = 14$ have infinitely many solutions?**
